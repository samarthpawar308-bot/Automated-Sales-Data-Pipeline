# Business Requirements

## 1. Business Problem

An e-commerce business generates data from customer orders, products, payments, sellers, and deliveries.

When this information is stored in separate files, manually combining and analysing it can become time-consuming.

The business needs a centralized and automated system to process its data and generate meaningful insights.

## 2. Proposed Solution

Develop an automated sales data pipeline that collects data, performs cleaning and validation, stores processed information in a database, and makes it available for business intelligence reporting.

## 3. Key Business Questions

The system should eventually answer:

1. What is the total sales revenue?
2. Which products generate the highest revenue?
3. Which product categories perform best?
4. Which regions generate the most orders?
5. What percentage of orders are delivered late?
6. What is the average order delivery time?
7. How many customers make repeat purchases?
8. How does sales performance change month over month?

## 4. Expected Outcomes

- Reduced manual data preparation.
- Consistent and validated business data.
- Centralized analytical database.
- Automated reporting workflow.
- Better visibility into sales and customer performance.
- Data-driven business decisions.

## 5. Planned Technology

Python, Pandas, PostgreSQL, SQL, REST APIs, and Power BI.
